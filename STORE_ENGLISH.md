## Detailed Description

**Stop losing ideas in endless tabs.**

MindTrace helps you capture thoughts the moment they appear — without breaking your flow.

### Highlight & Capture
Select any text on a webpage, add your own note, and save it with full context: page title, URL, and timestamp.

### Mind Garden
Browse, search, and revisit everything you've captured in a calm dashboard. Tag clouds, random reflection, and a timeline help your ideas resurface when you need them.

### Private by Design
Your data stays on your device. No account required. No cloud upload.

### Quick Shortcuts
- **Alt+Shift+M** — Save selected text
- **Alt+Shift+N** — Quick note without selecting text

Perfect for researchers, writers, founders, and anyone building a personal knowledge system.
